Rental Pokémon Price: 6000 > 8000
//Overall, I've been happy with this mechanic. It has all of the upsides I was hoping for: it helps the player through gyms 3 and 4, it gives them a last ditch hope if they suffer many early deaths, and it helps to diversify runs. That said, I want this mechanic to be niche. It's a black market; you visit it if you're desperate, not on a regular basis. At the moment, the mechanic is a bit stronger than I'd wanted it to be. This price increase brings it to the same price as an Earthquake TM, giving it a truly steep opportunity cost.
