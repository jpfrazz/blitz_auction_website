Gym Changes
-----------------------------------------------

Viola 1 Beautifly Mega Drain > Absorb
Viola 2 Surskit Bubble Beam > Bubble
//Mega Drain is not the move that makes early Beautifly so menacing, but while labbing the fight, I learned that Pokémon like Onix and Larvitar win this 1v1 if the Beautifly is using Absorb instead of Mega Drain. This change aims to add additional counters to Viola's ace without meaningfully weakening the fight's power level.


Brawly 1/2 Fire Punch > Brine
//Until Drain Punch was added, Brawly 1 and 2 were two of the easiest gyms in the game. I want these fights to remain formidable (I don't want to remove Drain Punch), but I also want to reduce the frequency with which the player enters these gyms with no Makuhita answer.
//So, this change looks to buff Bug types that should be good into Brawly by removing the threat of Fire-type secondary coverage. Now, your Pokémon that "should" be good into early Brawly are comparatively stronger, and your Leech-Seeding Grass types can make a larger contribution to the fight.


Tate & Liza 3-5 Baltoy Ancient Power > Bulldoze
Tate & Liza 6 Baltoy Ancient Power > Earthquake
//Leaving the Baltoy alive while focusing the other half of the fight is the "intended" strategy for Tate and Liza, but this fellow as it exists currently is just too weak.
//Bulldoze fits better with the theme of the team: it's unable to hit Natu/Solrock/Lunatone and slows both player Pokémon to set up for Solrock Rock Slide flinch. It now functions similarly to how Claydol functions in later Tate and Lizas. Overall, a more elegant way to adapt the fight to the earlier gyms.


The score bonus AI moves receive for reducing player speed has been reduced (from +2 to +1)
//In short, this means that the AI will be less fixated on choosing moves like Icy Wind (and Bulldoze) if it's slower.


Tate & Liza 6 Baltoy Self Destruct > Rapid Spin
Wattson 7/8 Golem Explosion > Earthquake
//I'm weighing various changes to give the AI a small chance to explode in any situation, but I'm holding off on them until the end of the Ever Grande Prix Season. In the meantime, I'm giving these two some real moves in the slots that are currently blanks.


All boss Pokémon now match the genders of their trainers
//Allows the player to anticipate which Pokémon they'll be able to Attract or Captivate
//Tate & Liza Natu/Xatu are female, Oranguru is male
//All of Tucker's Pokémon are female, preserving the E4's M-F-F-M splits
//Viola's Volbeat and Winona's Braviary are always male
//Genderless Pokémon remain genderless
