QoL
-----------------------------------------------

Added "ENEMY" menu option in battle
//Allows you to view the opponent's team, including stats and moves, mid battle. 
//It's a pet peeve of mine that nuzlocke hacks require you to have a separate tab of documentation open to see opponent teams; if the information is open access, you may as well be able to access it in game. It's also always bugged me that there's a useless "RUN" command on the screen in trainer battles. This change addresses both of my issues.

Holding the SELECT button while in the party menu now prevents Pokémon from attempting to evolve
//When delaying evolutions (for instance, when delaying Treecko's evolution until level 21 for Giga Drain), the player can now hold SELECT while using the Rare Candy in the party menu to prevent the Treecko from even attempting to evolve. This saves the time of having to manually stop the evolution animation each level.
//Notably, the game also runs an evolution check after you learn a move, so you'll need to be holding select when you choose to learn the move or not to prevent evolution.
//RIP Everstone

Removed the Pokeball pocket from the bag
//Pressing Left/Right in the item menu now skips the Pokeball section of the pack (a useless section in a game where you aren't allowed to catch Pokemon), allowing you to more quickly navigate the item menu.

Added the Deaths Count to the front of the trainer card
Added Steven & Wally Sprites to the back of the trainer card

Added a sparkle animation to key interaction points in the room
//Intended to subtly help new players take their first steps in Blitz without throwing in a disruptive text box
