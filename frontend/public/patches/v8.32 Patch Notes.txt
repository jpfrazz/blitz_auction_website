"Solo Mode"
-----------------------------------------------

Interact with the television in your room to enable Solo Mode!

In this mode...
-You have 80 points to spend
-Pokémon cost points to withdraw from the notebook
-All Pokémon have neutral IVs and natures (including Eevee)
-You're given 8 ability capsules, which you can use to choose your Pokemon's starting abilities
