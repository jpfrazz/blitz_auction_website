AI Changes
-----------------------------------------------

Stat-boosting moves that raise speed (Agility, Dragon Dance, Quiver Dance) receive a +3 boost to their score if...
 • The enemy Pokémon is currently slower than your Pokemon
 • The enemy Pokémon would be faster than your Pokémon at +1 speed
 • The enemy Pokémon isn't already at +1 speed
 • Your Pokémon hasn't revealed a speed-lowering move (like Icy Wind)
//Gives a chance for Viola's Masqueraine/Beautifly and Winona's Tropius/Altaria to boost up
//Both of these fights are relatively weak after gym 4. This gives them an extra wrinkle. I've never liked how underwhelming the Beautifly and Altaria are as aces--this forces you to respect them.
