Enemy Trainer AI Changes
-----------------------------------------------

If the enemy Pokémon would use Yawn, Attract, Swagger, Supersonic, Sweet Kiss, Confuse Ray, Teeter Dance, Sand Attack, Flash, or Smokescreen, 50% of the time, the move receives a -10 to its score.
//In other words, the enemy AI now uses these status moves half as often as it did previously
//Most of the time this change should feel good; situations where you'd be slowed down by Attract or Smokescreen will happen less frequently. That said, in some ways this will be a buff to the enemy trainers, who will now be more difficult to predict. Lum Berry set ups on Norman's Teeter Dancing Spinda or Sidney's Swaggering Shiftry, for example, are less consistent (you now risk the Shiftry opting to Leaf Tornado a few times before it goes for Swagger, dropping your accuracy and potentially botching your sweep).
//But this change wasn't ultimately made for either of these reasons. I just think that swapping back and forth between your Pokémon only for the enemy to continue spamming Yawn or Teeter Dance every other turn is unnatural and leads to ugly-looking gameplay. This change should make it feel more like you're fighting a real opponent and not a tedious robot.


If an opposing Pokémon knows Self Destruct or Explosion, once it's been on the field for at least 5 turns, starting on the 5th turn, it has a 50% chance to blow up each turn
//In short, exploding moves on enemy movesets are no longer "blanks"
//The 50% chance to blow up is only if the opposing Pokémon can be affected by Explosion; it won't knowingly explode into a ghost type

As a reminder, the full list of enemy Pokémon that can explode include:
Roxanne 5-8 Graveler/Golem
Wattson 5-8 Electrode
Tate&Liza 5-6 Baltoy
Maxie Torkoal
Brandon Regirock
