Pokemon controlled by Tatsugiri's Commander ability can now land critical hits
//Extended Commentary Start
Instead of starting the fight with Dondozo, Tatsugiri now leads with a Flip Turn Luvdisc by its side.

Luvdisc starts the fight by Safeguarding the first turn and Flip Turning the second. Can you kill the Tatsugiri before Luvdisc Flip Turns? Can you Fake Out or Encore the Luvdisc to buy yourself extra time? Can you switch to bait Flip Turn into a Water Absorb Pokemon, causing Luvdisc to fail to swap out?

Keep in mind, you'll have time for two Eevee Quick Attacks before the Luvdisc Flip Turns, or a Tail Whip into a Quick Attack. Alongside a physical attacker who Eevee outspeeds, Tail Whip > a partner's physical attack > Quick Attack usually KOs the Tatsugiri before Dondozo has a chance to appear.

Even if you fail to kill the Tatsugiri, though, Dondozo with Order Up is doing much less damage than it was with Waterfall. While it might score a critical hit, a crit Order Up won't one-shot Eevee from full at +0. Luvdisc also outspeeds Tatsugiri, meaning that if it gets off a Flip Turn into Dondozo, that'll be the only attack for their turn.

I'm hoping that this makes the fight more intuitive. Ideally, seeing the lead Flip Turn Luvdisc with the Order Up Dondozo in the back presents a puzzle that invites the player to craft a solution.
//Extended Commentary End

Flareon Evolution Move: Ember > Flame Charge

//Extended Commentary Start
Oh Flareon, you're the last Eeveelution to be chosen again! As you know, I don't usually mind Pokémon being weak (if all Pokémon are worth 2500, the draft loses its complexity--it's the range in power that makes deciding what to bid on so tricky). But Flareon isn't in the draft, and the recurring decision of which Eeveelution to choose after gym 4 would be more interesting if Flareon were closer to the rest of the pack.

Not that this makes Flareon a highly sought after Eeveelution or anything. But Flame Charge is perfect for Flareon: a physical fire-type attack that patches up its low Speed and helps enable the aggressive, Guts-boosted gameplan it wants to play for.

I've wanted to give Flareon access to Flame Charge for ages via TM, but I don't like the implication of giving Pokémon like Dachsbun the ability to setup Atk/SpAtk/Speed pass just as well as Ninjask, or already high-tier fires like Chandelure and Skeledirge a way to buff their own speed. So, I've settled for the ugly decision to modify its learnset outright. This won't become a routine, but it makes too much sense for this specific Pokémon.
//Extended Commentary End
