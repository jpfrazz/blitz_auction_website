+Basculin

//Basculin evolves when the total levels of all your fainted Pokemon is 100 or more
//For example, if you've lost a level 15, 24, 29, and 32 Pokémon, your Basculin can evolve into Basculegion. Or, if you've lost a level 35 Pokémon and a level 58 Pokémon, it can evolve. Or, if you've just finished sacrificing eight level 15 Pokémon to Blackbelt Nob, it can evolve before the first gym. Don't get any ideas, Manthief.
//Fainted rental Pokémon do count toward the total, even after they've been reclaimed by Renter NPC

//Basculegion's Pokedex entry reads "This Pokémon is cloaked in the souls of its comrades who perished during a punishing journey to the river of their birth." This is so sad. The idea of the original evolution mechanic (taking 294 HP of recoil damage without fainting) is that this is the one Basculin who lived, and all of the others who died are getting sort of absorbed into it (hence the ghost typing). We can't do the recoil evolution mechanic here (I guess we could, but that's not an evolution mechanic that creates the gameplay Blitz is going for). So, it has a new one that I think stays thematically appropriate.

//And no, its egg move is not Last Respects. No stepping on Houndstone's toes!
