Pokemon Changes
-----------------------------------------------

Togetic learns Fairy Wind on Evolution (level 10)
Pikachu learns Electro Ball on Evolution (level 10)
Jigglypuff and Clefairy learn Disarming voice on Evolution (level 10)
Marill learns Bubble Beam on Evolution (level 10)
//Togepi doesn't learn Fairy Wind by level up, but Togetic does at 14. Because Togepi comes at level 15, it was missing a chance at learning this move.
//Once upon a time, Pokémon came out of the notebook at level 5, and you leveled them up to 15 in your room before starting your run. This was tedious, so we switched to having Pokémon come at the opening level cap with the best possible learnset for that point.
//All of which is to say, Togepi "should" have the option to learn Fairy Wind, since if it came at 5, you'd evolve it at 10 and learn the move at 14 on Togetic. We'll add them as evolution moves to remedy the problem. This is the case for all of the above additions.


Tentacool learns Water Gun @ 1
Gabite learns Bite @ 27
//Yet another pairs of backports from future generations to our generation 7 learnsets


Blitzle Egg Move: Double Kick > High Horsepower
//I don't like how same-y all of the bottom-tier electric types feel. This doesn't make Blitzle good, but it at least gives it something to set it apart. I'm cheating with this (it's not actually an Egg move for Blitzle in generation 7), but other Pokémon do have High Horsepower as an egg move, so it doesn't feel too out of place.

Fletchling Egg Move: Tailwind > Blaze Kick
//Cheating again in the name of getting an iconic gen 3 move onto a Pokemon where it's particularly fitting (and useful).

Riolu Egg Move: Meteor Mash > Sky Uppercut
//Speaking of cheating, Riolu doesn't have Meteor Mash as an egg move in generation 7. The level 48 Meteor Mash it gets in later generations has already been backported, so I'm comfortable making him wait until gym 8 to learn that move.


Bombardier Ability: Stork's Blessing > Big Pecks / Keen Eye
//Recently, it was discovered that Stork's Blessing doesn't work and produces corrupt egg. This bug was introduced when the egg starting containing just 4 Pokemon instead of 19. The fact that this bug has been in the game for two months and we only just now caught it suggests to me that it's time to retire the ability. I like Stork's Blessing, but it's an obscure mechanic that became 10x more obscure when Bombirdier moved to being a rental Pokémon. Maybe some day we'll revisit custom abilities, but for the time being I'm comfortable sticking to the defaults.

Mega Scrafty Ability > Moxie
//Similar to Staraptor, this thing's mega ability isn't going to be Intimidate. The current interaction is janky, so I'm giving it a filler ability while we wait to learn its official one.
